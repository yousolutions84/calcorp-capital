Investment Services

  • Strategy
  • Objectives

In today’s business environment a secure sale undertaken in a confidential manner is an attractive option to sellers who have generated wealth through their business and are seeking to secure a future for their management. We are able to move quickly through due diligence to financial close. Our transaction experience affords us with the necessary skills to implement a professional and convenient acquisition strategy. This strategy is aimed at maintaining the integrity of the target business.

We back profitable, cash generative companies with proven management and established market positions.

We invest together with management. Our experience enables us to provide our investees with access to international capabilities. There is no "one size fits all" approach. We will explore each acquisition on its merits.
