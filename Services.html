"Execution with Integrity"

Calcorp Capital Ltd, London, focuses exclusively on International Trade and Project Finance activities and services – from normal trading, structured financing, forfeiting and invoice discounting, through to Project financing and management.

Our Directors, located in Australia, Europe and UK have in excess of 100 years international trading and trade & project finance experience.

Contact us via info@calcorpcapital.com and we will respond to your inquiry with 24 hours.


Our approach is to provide you with a totally independent and integrated service. When providing advice we do not earn commissions or fees from any third parties. That way we avoid conflicts of interest. Whether its in an advisory role or as the buyer of your business we work along side you and your team. We learn about and understand your business and strategies, then we deliver solutions that meet your objectives.

Calcorp provides services in three primary sectors;

  1. Business advisory covering general business support in terms of strategy, business development and international markets;
  2. Private Investment in genuinely retiring small businesses between $5m and $50m; and
  3. Small Business tools and advice.
